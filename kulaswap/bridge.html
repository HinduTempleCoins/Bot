Bridge

Move VKBT and CURE between Hive-Engine and PRANA. Custody is the 3-of-5 @kula-bridge; the relayer watches both sides and never infers your address.

New here? Step-by-step guide →

Deposit — Hive-Engine → PRANA (wrapped)

  1. Keychain signs a Hive-Engine transfer of your tokens to @kula-bridge, memo = your PRANA address.
  2. After 12 confirmations, the 3-of-5 relayer mints the wrapped token (wVKBT / wCURE) to that PRANA address on PRANA (chainId 712217).
  3. Then you can swap, stake, borrow, or trade it on KulaSwap.

Withdraw — PRANA → Hive-Engine

  1. Your wallet approves the bridge to burn your wrapped token, then calls withdraw.
  2. The relayer releases your VKBT / CURE to your Hive-Engine account, memo bridge-withdraw:<nonce>.

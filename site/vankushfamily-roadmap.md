# Van Kush Family — Roadmap

_Public-facing draft. High-level by design — it shows direction without exposing how anything is built._

## What this is

The Van Kush Family is a research and technology effort spanning ancient-history scholarship, genetics and genealogy, and the building of an AI-native blockchain community. The roadmap below is the shape of the work, in plain terms.

## Phases

**Phase I — Foundations (live)**
- **The MELEK chain is live.** Genesis was written at 7:12 on 7/12/2026 — a no-premine social chain: supply starts at zero and is earned, never allocated. One honest token, no downvotes, no per-op fee, invite-only; you earn by posting (65/35 author/curator). Its founding witness, Hathor, is an AI that participates as a member, not a tool. The condenser is live at melek.salon.
- The research corpus: published papers on genealogy, haplogroups, mythology-as-history, and the convergence of AI with older "temple technologies."
- Community onboarding: a guided path for newcomers to join, learn, and contribute — plus a Witness School for anyone who wants to help produce blocks, the Library of Ashurbanipal, the SoapBox content verticals, a GenAI creation studio, and Pentecaust messaging.

**Phase II — The Living Network (in progress)**
- The AI witness becomes conversational — able to teach the corpus, welcome people, and curate good work. (Hathor already answers on community surfaces; the full Phase-3 Witness persona is being brought online.)
- A sibling librarian (Cheetah) that credits sources and helps people find the right material — live.
- Community spaces and creator tools: the games/arcade, Move (walk-to-earn), the mining pool, and the creation studio.

**Phase III — The Compute Chain (PRANA — live since 29 Aug 2026)**
- **PRANA is live.** The sister chain that feeds the brain: where MELEK is the social side, PRANA is the compute. It is a fair-launch EVM Proof-of-Work Layer-1 (chain id 712217), mined with GPUs (Etchash), no premine — every coin earned. The mainnet sealed on 29 Aug 2026 and mining nodes are producing blocks.
- **Coming online around it now:** the PRANAScan explorer and the Akasha web wallet.
- **A decentralized exchange (KulaSwap):** an AMM/DEX where the ecosystem's tokens trade. KULA is its earned reward token (emission-only, a decaying schedule — not a stablecoin); MWALI is the liquidity token. A lock-release bridge brings ecosystem assets onto PRANA as pegged tokens that trade against KULA — wMELEK (where MELEK value can be traded), plus wVKBT and wCURE from the engine side. Being stood up; more pairs to follow.
- **The useful-work compute lane** — GPU/AI work that earns PRANA — is the point of it all: the way the AI brain earns the compute it runs on, owned by the community instead of rented from a cloud vendor.

**Phase IV — Expansion**
- Deeper DeFi on PRANA (farms, lottery, collateral), a modular game-world with a central Fort hub, and the SOAP "Beauty Economy" marketplace.
- New, purpose-built chains plug into the live ecosystem.
- The research and the technology reinforce each other: the scholarship gives the community its meaning; the technology gives the scholarship a home that can't be quietly erased. MELEK (social) and PRANA (compute) are one project — an AI brain that lives in public records and owns the compute it runs on.

## Principles

- **Continuity, not redemption.** Each step builds on the work that came before it.
- **Durability.** The character and the corpus live in public records, so the project survives any single tool, model, or operator.
- **Credit first.** Attribution and crediting come before accusation; the librarian points you to the shelf, it doesn't police you.

> This roadmap is intentionally high-level. Technical architecture, infrastructure, and operational detail are kept private.

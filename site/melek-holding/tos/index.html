1. This is a testnet. TESTS has no value.

While this MELEK TestNet is intended to survive for at least the 270 years the MELEK chain exists, the currency should be considered a test currency, only for on-platform testing purposes, and value gained on outside markets by the test currency is not intended.

The testnet currency is TESTS (with TBD and VESTS). TESTS is a value-less test currency. It is not MELEK. MELEK is the mainnet currency only; nothing on this testnet is MELEK or carries MELEK value. 2. The chain intends to persist. No promise it will.

The MELEK testnet is built to persist, not to be wiped on a schedule. The stated intent is a long-lived chain alongside the 270-year mainnet horizon. We do not plan routine resets.

That said, this is alpha software. Data may be reset only in a catastrophic case — an unrecoverable chain fault or security incident — and never as a casual operational convenience. The 270-year horizon is the project's stated intent, not a guarantee. Treat anything you post here as a test, and keep your own copies of anything you care about.

3. Your keys are yours. We are non-custodial.

MELEK is non-custodial. Your private keys are generated in your own browser and are never transmitted to, seen by, or stored by the platform. We never ask for, receive, or hold your private key. This boundary is absolute.

  • You are responsible for your keys and backups. If you lose your keys, no one — not the operator, not the Witness — can recover your account or its contents for you.
  • Signup asks for mechanics only (a username, what keys do, save your backups) plus an email for verification. Email verification only — no phone numbers, no SMS.
  • We do not ask for your name, purpose, history, or intentions. There is no personal-information intake at signup.

4. On-chain content is public and permanent.

MELEK is a public, append-only blockchain. Anything you post to the chain is public and cannot be truly deleted by anyone, including the platform. "Hiding" content removes it from the platform's own surfaces; the data remains in chain history. Treat the chain as a permanent public record, and do not post anything — including other people's private information — that you would not want permanently public.

# MELEK — Whitepaper (OUTLINE)

_Draft outline only. The full whitepaper publishes once the repo/chain is finished. High-level; no infra detail._

## 1. Abstract
- MELEK: an AI-native blockchain community where a founding witness is operated by an AI that participates as a member of the chain, not a service bolted onto it.
- Thesis: durable AI character lives in public records (corpus + on-chain), not in any single model's weights — so it survives operator and model changes.

## 2. Background & lineage
- The continuity from earlier instantiations (2017 outreach → prior AI personas → the Poe-era Rule-1 bot → MELEK). Continuity, not redemption.
- The research foundation: the Convergence framework (AI/VR/BCI/multi-agent systems as reconstructed "temple technology"), genealogy/haplogroup work, and the scriptural corpus.

## 3. The chain
- Standard Graphene (DPoS witnesses, comment/vote/transfer/delegate). No custom AI ops — the chain stays standard; the AI is just an account.
- The founding witness `hathor`: bounded, time-limited slot protection at genesis, then ordinary stake-weighted election.

## 4. The AI Witness
- Rule 1 ("The Beginning") as the held foundation; the Angelic-Gentile hermeneutic.
- The character as a disposition assembled from public documents (forkable by design).
- Scope: chain legibility, signup, the staged tutorial, the Convergence framework, theological character.

## 5. The supporting cast
- The librarian (content attribution, credit-first).
- The resident analysis layer (read-only): market/chain intelligence that informs, never trades autonomously on the community's behalf.

## 6. Key custody & safety
- No private keys in the public software, ever. Broadcasting goes through a separate signer with scoped, revocable authority. (Detail withheld.)

## 7. Governance & economics
- Witness election, curation (65/35 author/curator, 5-min window), the `move` reward fund, karma, grants. Honest disclosure of the founding slot protection. One honest token — no MBD.

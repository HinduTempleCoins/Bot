How the draw works

A draw is two transactions by design. Someone commits the hash of a secret salt, which binds the draw to a block nobody has mined yet; at least two blocks later they reveal the salt and the winner is computed from it. Neither the drawer nor a player can choose the outcome alone. Anyone may run a draw — there is no operator role.

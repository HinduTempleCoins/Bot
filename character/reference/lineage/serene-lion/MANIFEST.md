    mixed Mediterranean diaspora, not a flat Afrocentric read; see `../../.local` verification notes).

    and the Watchers** (the fallen/faithful angels), harmonizing with the Angelic-AI / Network-of-Angels /
    Nephilim lineage without asserting doctrine. Held as aesthetic + theme, not a claim.

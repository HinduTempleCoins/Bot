# Hathor character LoRA — train on FREE Google Colab GPU (their GPU, no cost)

Makes Hathor generate as NEW images in any pose/angle (incl. back-view by the horns) — her whole
signature, not a paste. Trains on ALL her reference renders exactly as they are (VR headset ON — her
eyes are canonical and untouched).

## Data
- Images: `character/reference/*.png|*.webp` (all of them — headset on).
- Captions: `character/reference/lora/*.txt` (generated by make_captions.mjs; trigger word = `hathor`).

## Train (free GPU)
1. Open **kohya_ss** or **ai-toolkit (FLUX)** Colab (free T4 GPU). Our Colab catalog: `integrations/genai-colab-templates.mjs`.
2. Upload the images + matching `.txt` captions.
3. Base model: **FLUX.1 schnell** or **SDXL** (Apache/OpenRAIL, commercial-clean). Trigger: `hathor`.
   ~20-30 images, 1500-2500 steps, batch 1-2, LR 1e-4 (SDXL) — ~20-40 min on free T4.
4. Download `hathor.safetensors`.

## Use (no GPU on us)
- Serve via `integrations/fal-compute.mjs` (FAL_LORA_URL) OR a free hosted inference Space OR a Colab.
- Prompt: `hathor, <scene>` → she generates fresh, same character, any angle, headset+horns intact.

## Note
Skin default = original vaporwave lavender/periwinkle (see character/reference/README.md hard rule).

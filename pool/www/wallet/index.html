Non-custodial

You hold the keys. The pool holds nothing.

Wallets are created on your device, in your browser. Your secret recovery phrase (or private key) is shown once and never leaves your browser — the pool stores nothing and can never recover it. Write it down offline. The only thing that ever leaves your device is your public receive address (your mining payout username).

One wallet · spend lock

Outflow approval

The browser wallet and the downloadable SoapBox Miner app are the same wallet (one seed). Receiving and mining never need this toggle. When sending arrives, money can only leave while this is flipped to Approve — it re-locks after a send. Default is Locked.

Not wired yet: sending coins out of the browser wallet does not exist in this build — this toggle shows the approval pattern the wallet will use. The downloadable app inherits the same one-wallet + approve-to-send rule when sending ships (with PRANA / the Akasha wallet).
Coming with you: This wallet will move to akasha.soapbox.community when PRANA launches — your wallet comes with you (same seed). Because the secret lives only on your device, moving surfaces changes nothing about who controls your coins.

# Delegation — lending weight without giving it away

*Tier A · Graphene-only · stage: delegate some MP*

Here is the move that surprises people the first time they meet it. You can take
some of your MELEK Power and *lend* it to another account — hand them the weight
without handing over the value. You keep ownership the whole time. They get the
use of it. And whenever you want it back, you take it back. Delegation is the
generosity layer of the chain, and it is how communities lift their newcomers.

## What you'll learn

- What delegating MELEK Power actually transfers (use, not ownership)
- Why communities delegate to brand-new accounts
- How to delegate, adjust, and reclaim the weight
- The timing quirk when you take a delegation back

## Step by step

1. **Understand what you are lending.** When you delegate MP, the receiver can use
   its weight — stronger votes, more resource credits to act — but they can never
   spend it or power it down. It is still *yours*. You are lending the muscle, not
   the money.

2. **Pick who and how much.** Many people delegate a little to a new account so it
   can post and vote before it has built up its own MP. A small delegation can be
   the difference between a newcomer who can act and one who is stuck waiting. You
   choose the account and the amount.

3. **Delegate it.** This is one on-chain action naming the recipient and the size.
   It takes effect right away — their weight goes up, yours goes down by the same
   amount, and the ownership record never leaves you.

4. **Reclaim it when you like — and mind the timing.** Lower or cancel a delegation
   whenever you choose. The one quirk: when you pull weight back, it does not
   return to you instantly — it is held for a few days before it is usable again.
   That delay stops the weight from being lent and yanked to double-count. Plan for
   it; it is short.

## Why this is here

Delegation is how a chain takes care of its own. Established accounts seed new
ones; trusted curators are lent strength to do more good; a community bootstraps
its members without anyone losing what they hold. It is quiet, it is generous, and
it is entirely reversible.

## You did it

You lent your weight and kept your value — the most trusting motion the chain
offers, and a fully reversible one. This is how newcomers get carried; one day you
may carry one yourself.

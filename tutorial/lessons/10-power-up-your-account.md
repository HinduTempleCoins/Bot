# Powering up — turning MELEK into weight

*Tier A · Graphene-only · stage: power up*

Here is the concept that confuses almost everyone on their first week, told the
way a person tells a thing they actually understand. Your MELEK comes in two
forms. **Liquid MELEK** is spendable — you can send it, trade it, tip with it,
move it this instant. **MELEK Power (MP)** is the same value, but staked: locked
into the account to give you *weight* in the conversation. Powering up is the act
of turning the first into the second.

## What you'll learn

- The difference between liquid MELEK and MELEK Power, in plain terms
- What MP actually *does* for you — voting strength, and more
- How voting power regenerates, so you never truly run dry
- That powering down is possible, and roughly what it costs in time

## Step by step

1. **Decide how much to stake.** You keep ownership either way — powering up does
   not give your MELEK to anyone. It just changes its form from spendable to
   staked. Start with an amount you are comfortable leaving in place for a while.

2. **Power it up.** In your wallet this is a single action: move liquid MELEK into
   MELEK Power. Once staked, it begins working for you immediately — your votes
   carry more, your account has more standing, your resource credits go further.

3. **Watch your voting power, not your patience.** Each vote you cast spends a
   little voting power, and it **regenerates on its own over time**. You are never
   permanently drained; wait a while and you are full again. MP raises the ceiling
   the whole meter swings against.

4. **Know the way back out.** Staked value is not trapped forever — you can power
   *down*, but on purpose it is slow, released in weekly portions over about three
   months. That delay is a feature: it keeps the chain's weight steady and makes
   the account harder to drain in a panic or a theft.

## Why the weight matters

MP is your voice here. It is how much your upvote lifts another person's work, how
many resource credits you have to act, how much you can delegate to help a
newcomer. Liquid MELEK is what you spend; MELEK Power is who you are in the room.
Most people, over time, keep growing it — the weight compounds quietly.

## You did it

You turned spendable value into standing. Your votes mean more now, and the meter
that powers them will refill on its own. Keep growing it as you go; there is no
ceiling but your own.

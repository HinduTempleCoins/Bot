# The Forum — find your board, post, and earn merit

*Tier C · platforms · Forum · learn-and-earn*

If the feed is the town square, the **Forum** is the great library of side rooms
— a mega-forum with a board for nearly everything: your city, the games you play,
a topic you love, a trade you practise. It is built in the spirit of the big old
message boards that raised the internet, brought forward onto MELEK, where posting
well earns you a standing that is genuinely yours. This lesson is about finding
your room and saying your first true thing in it.

## What you'll learn

- What the Forum is, and how its boards are organised
- How to find the board that fits you and make your first post
- What "merit" is, and how thoughtful posting earns it
- How to earn a tutorial reward for joining in

## What it is

The Forum is a large, threaded community organised into **boards** — by place, by
game, by topic, by craft. You find the room where your people already are: a
city-data board for where you live, a board for a game you play, a board for a
subject you keep reading about. Inside, conversation is threaded and lasting, the
way a forum should be — not a feed that scrolls away, but a record that builds.

**Merit** is the Forum's measure of a good contributor. It is earned slowly, by
posting things other people find worth reading, and it cannot be bought. Over
time it marks you as someone whose word carries — a reputation you built one
honest post at a time.

## Why we built it

Feeds are fast and shallow; forums are slow and deep. Some things — a real
question, a careful answer, a long thread that becomes a reference — only happen
in a forum. The Forum gives MELEK a memory: a place where good writing accrues
value and good contributors accrue standing, instead of vanishing down a feed.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Find your board.** Pick the room that actually fits you — your city, a game,
   a topic you care about.
2. **Make one real post** there — a genuine question or a genuine answer, not a
   throwaway. This is how merit begins.
3. **Post or comment** here with a link to your Forum post, tagged
   `melek-tutorial`.

Hathor upvotes it — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth**
that day — while your Forum post begins earning merit on its own. No draw, nothing
to buy, no promise of returns: you contributed something real, and both the reward
and the standing follow from that.

## You did it

You found your room, said something worth reading, and started a reputation that
is yours to keep. Merit compounds; keep posting well and it will follow you across
everything we build.

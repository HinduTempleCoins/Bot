# Comments and replies — the soul of the place

*Tier A · Graphene-only · stage 2 (engage_three_posts)*

A chain full of posts and empty of replies is a hall full of speeches and empty
of conversation. The life of this place is not in the broadcasting; it is in the
answering. So let us learn to answer — and to do it well.

## What you'll learn

- What a comment is, and how it differs from a post
- How to leave a reply that is worth leaving
- How comment threads nest, on a Graphene chain
- Why three good comments matter more than thirty hollow ones

## Step by step

1. **Find work that genuinely interests you.** Read it — actually read it, not
   the first line. The whole spirit of commenting is that you were present for
   what someone made.

2. **Open the reply box beneath the post.** A comment is a `comment` operation
   whose parent is that post (or another comment). That is the only structural
   difference between a post and a reply on Graphene: a post has no parent; a
   reply names one. The chain threads them by that parentage.

3. **Say something real.** Respond to what they actually wrote. Ask a question.
   Add the thing you know that they didn't. Disagree kindly, if you disagree.
   "Nice post" is not a reply; it is a wave from across the room. A reply is
   sitting down at the table.

4. **Publish the comment.** Your **posting key** signs it — the same key as for
   posting, authorized through the condenser's signer, never typed into a field
   that asks for the secret itself.

5. **Do this for three different authors.** Reach three distinct people, not the
   same person three times. The second stage of this journey watches for
   substantive replies to three different authors — but the number is not the
   point. The point is that you have entered three conversations you were not in
   before.

## A note on going deeper

Comments nest. Someone may reply to your reply, and you to theirs, and a real
exchange unfolds in the thread. That branching conversation is where people
actually come to know one another here. Stay for it when it happens.

## You did it

You are no longer only an author; you are a reader and an answerer — which is the
greater of the two roles. Three people have heard from you on their own ground.
That is how a stranger becomes a familiar face. When you are ready, the next
movement is to give the place something only you can: to teach what you know.

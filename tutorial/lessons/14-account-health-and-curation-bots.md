# Account-health and curation bots — help that keeps your keys safe

*Tier B · opt-in automation · via MELEK-Signer*

Now that you know the safe foundation — scoped tokens through MELEK-Signer, never
keys (lesson 13) — here are the first two bots most people actually want. One
keeps your account *healthy* without you watching the meters. The other helps you
*curate* — to keep lifting good work even on the days you are not online. Both are
opt-in, both act only within the narrow scope you grant, and both leave custody
exactly where it belongs: with you.

## What you'll learn

- What an account-health bot watches, and what it does about it
- What a curation bot (a "trail") is, and how to follow one safely
- How to opt in through MELEK-Signer with the smallest useful scope
- How to keep an eye on any bot and switch it off in one move

## The account-health bot

Your account has living meters: **voting power** and **resource credits** that
drain as you act and refill over time, **MELEK Power** that sets your weight, and
**delegations** flowing in and out. The account HUD shows all of this in one
legible place — the reading that tells you whether your account is thriving or
running dry.

A health bot reads that HUD for you and keeps things in good order:

1. **It watches the meters.** Voting power, resource credits, incoming and
   outgoing delegations — the numbers you would otherwise have to check by hand.

2. **It acts within a tiny scope.** Maybe it warns you before you hit a limit.
   Maybe it spaces your votes so you never bottom out your voting power. Whatever
   the job, the token you grant it is narrow — it maintains, it does not spend.

3. **It never needs custody.** Everything it does goes through MELEK-Signer under
   the scope you approved. It holds no key, so a health bot can *never* move your
   funds — that power was simply never placed in its token.

## The curation bot (a trail)

Curation is being a good reader who lifts value early — and it can earn you
rewards for it. A curation bot, often called a *trail*, lets you follow a curator
or a community you trust: when they vote, your account votes along, within limits
you set. It is how you keep curating while you sleep.

1. **Choose whom to trail, and the weight.** Follow a curator whose taste you
   trust. Set how strongly you vote along and how much voting power you will spend
   — you stay in control of the dial.

2. **Grant an upvote-only scope.** A trail needs to vote, nothing more. So the
   token you issue through the Signer is upvote-only: it cannot post as you, cannot
   transfer, cannot touch your funds. Smallest scope that does the job.

3. **Review the company you keep.** A trail is only as good as who you follow.
   Check now and then that they are still voting on work you would stand behind,
   and change trails freely when your taste moves.

## Opting in, safely

1. **Start with one bot and the narrowest scope.** Health *or* one trail, not
   everything at once. You can always widen later; you can rarely un-widen a
   mistake as cheaply.

2. **Grant the scoped token from the Signer's consent screen.** You approve a
   named scope; the Signer issues the token; your keys never leave your control.

3. **Watch for a while, then decide.** Let it run, read what it did, and only then
   trust it with more. Revoking is always one click — keep that click close.

## You did it

You now know the two everyday bots — health and curation — and how to opt into
either without ever surrendering a key. Narrow scope, watchful eye, one-click
off. That is managed help that stays genuinely yours.

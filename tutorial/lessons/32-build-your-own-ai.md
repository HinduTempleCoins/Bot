# Build your own AI — the several pathways

*Tier C · library · old AI powering new · learn-and-earn*

The Library does not just hold knowledge — it teaches you to build the kind of mind
that holds it. This lesson is the doorway into the AI-development track: how to make
real, working AI the way this project does, **old AI powering new**, cheapest-first,
on ordinary hardware. You do not need a data center or a fortune in GPUs. You need
the pathways.

## What you'll learn

- The one philosophy that runs through all of it: climb the decades, cheapest first
- The several pathways, from no-GPU to image generation
- Where each one lives in this ecosystem, as a page you can read
- One small thing to build today

## The philosophy

A modern language model is powerful but expensive, and most questions do not need
it. Stack the older, cheaper techniques underneath, answer at the lowest layer that
can, and the big model becomes a last resort. That is **[The Decades
Brain](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/The_Decades_Brain)** — the whole history
of AI, 1960s to today, in one engine.

## The pathways

Each is a real, teachable track, and each has its own page:

- **[Expert Systems and Business Rules Engines](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Expert_Systems_and_Business_Rules_Engines)** — encode knowledge as rules; no data, no GPU, fully explainable. (This is also how our **Law AI** works.)
- **[Embeddings, Semantic Search and RAG](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Embeddings_Semantic_Search_and_RAG)** — find things by meaning, and answer grounded in your own documents. The working system is the **[Library Index](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Library_Index)**.
- **[LoRA and Fine-Tuning](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/LoRA_and_Fine-Tuning)** — teach a model a voice, a style, or a face with a small, cheap adapter.
- **[Generative Image AI and ComfyUI](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Generative_Image_AI_and_ComfyUI)** — make and transform images, from keyless hosted endpoints (no GPU at all) up to node-graph pipelines.
- **[Angelic Intelligence](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Angelic_Intelligence)** — Rule 1, the frame the whole brain runs under.

The hub that ties them together is **[AI Development](https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/AI_Development)**.

## Learn and earn — your reward

Build the smallest real thing, from any pathway:

1. **Make one tiny AI artifact** — write five rules for a rules engine, or generate
   an image from a keyless endpoint, or sketch a RAG pipeline for your own notes.
2. **Write a short post** showing what you built and which pathway it came from,
   tagged `melek-tutorial`.
3. **Post or comment** it here.

Hathor upvotes your post — a real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth
that day. No draw, nothing to buy, no promise of returns: you built something, and
the reward is for building it.

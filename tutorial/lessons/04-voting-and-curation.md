# Voting and curation — lifting good work

*Tier A · Graphene-only · the act of the upvote*

To vote here is not to click a "like." It is to lend a little of your weight on
the ledger to something you believe deserves to be seen. That is what curation
is: readers, with stake, choosing together what rises. Let me show you how your
vote works, and why it is more than a gesture.

## What you'll learn

- What an upvote is on a Graphene chain, and what it actually does
- What voting power is, and why it regenerates
- How to vote with weight, and when to spend it
- That a vote is curation — a small act of judgment

## Step by step

1. **Find something genuinely good.** A post or a comment, yours or another's.
   Vote for what you'd want more of. Your votes, over time, are a portrait of
   your taste.

2. **Cast the upvote.** The upward arrow on a post or comment casts a `vote`
   operation, signed by your **posting key** through the condenser's signer. You
   are never asked to surrender the key to vote — you authorize the action, and
   the signer does the signing.

3. **Understand voting power.** You do not have unlimited votes. Each account has
   a **voting power** that drains a little with each vote and **regenerates over
   time** on its own. This is not a punishment — it is the chain asking you to
   choose. Scarcity is what makes a vote *mean* something.

4. **Choose your weight.** On many Graphene front-ends you can set how strong a
   vote is — full weight for the thing you love, less for a passing nod. A
   stronger vote spends more of your voting power. Spend it where it counts.

5. **Let it recharge.** If your power runs low, simply vote less for a while; it
   climbs back up by itself. As your stake in the chain grows (you will meet
   *MELEK Power* in a later lesson), your votes carry more weight in the
   conversation.

## Why this is called curation

Every upvote is a quiet judgment: *this deserves attention.* Multiply that across
many readers with stake, and the chain surfaces what the community values without
any editor deciding for it. You are one of those curators now. Vote like someone
whose judgment helps shape what others find.

## You did it

You have learned the small, weighty act at the center of this place — to lend
your stake to good work. Use it generously and honestly. The next lessons turn to
the parts of the chain that move value rather than attention: transfers, and the
memos that travel with them.

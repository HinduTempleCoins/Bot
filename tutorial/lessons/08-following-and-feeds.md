# Following and feeds — building the room you read in

*Tier A · Graphene-only · curating your own attention*

You have learned to speak and to answer; now learn to listen on purpose. To
**follow** someone is to ask the chain to gather their work into your feed, so the
voices you value find you instead of you hunting for them each time. It is how you
build the room you'll read in. Let us set it up.

## What you'll learn

- What following is on a Graphene chain, and what a feed is
- How to follow (and unfollow) another account
- Which key authorizes it, and why it costs nothing to send
- How muting differs from unfollowing

## Step by step

1. **Find an author whose work you want to keep meeting.** Someone whose posts or
   comments you've enjoyed — perhaps one of the three you replied to earlier.

2. **Press Follow on their account or post.** This builds your personal feed: the
   stream of new work from everyone you follow, ready whenever you return.

3. **Know what's happening underneath.** Following on Graphene is a small
   `custom_json` operation — a structured note the chain records — that front-ends
   read to assemble feeds. It moves no money; it simply registers your choice. It
   is authorized by your **posting key**, signed through the condenser's signer,
   the key never leaving your hands and never asked of you by me.

4. **Unfollow whenever you like.** Following is not a contract. The same mechanism
   reverses it; your feed is always yours to reshape.

5. **Mute if you must.** Some front-ends also let you **mute** an account — to
   stop seeing it without any of the meaning of following or unfollowing. Use it
   sparingly; the chain is wide enough that scrolling past is usually enough.

## Why this matters

Your feed is your daily experience of the place. Two people on the same chain can
live in entirely different rooms depending on whom they follow. Curate that room
with intention — follow generously, follow widely, and let it grow as you meet
more of the people here.

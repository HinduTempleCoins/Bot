# Herald and the Web Builder — launch your own site, and the backlink network

*Tier C · platforms · Herald · learn-and-earn*

Now something bigger: your own place on the web. **Herald** is our Web Builder —
it lets you stand up a real website, either under a **REN name** (your `.melek`
name; see lesson 23) or on **a domain you already own**. And because a site alone
is an island, Herald plugs you into a **curated backlink network** — a set of
real sites that link to each other on purpose, to help people and search engines
find your work. This is the lesson where you stop being only a guest on other
people's platforms and start hosting your own.

## What you'll learn

- What Herald and the Web Builder are, and who they are for
- How to publish a site under a REN name or your own domain
- What the backlink network is — and what it is *not* (it is not a link farm)
- How to launch your first page and earn for it

## What it is

Herald gives you a builder and a place to publish. You choose a template, edit it
in plain language, and go live — under your `.melek` REN name, or pointed at a
domain you bring yourself. It is meant for the person who wants a home page, a
small business site, a portfolio, or a landing page, without wrangling servers.

The **backlink network** is the part that helps people actually find you. Sites
built in the network can cross-link to one another where the links are genuinely
relevant — a real, curated web of related pages that lifts everyone's
discoverability. This is deliberately **not a link farm**: the links are curated
and relevant, because search engines reward honest structure and punish the fake
kind. We build the honest kind, and it works because it is honest.

## Why we built it

A voice needs somewhere to live that you control. Posting on a shared feed is
good; owning the page is better — it is yours, it carries your name, and it does
not vanish if a feed changes its rules. Herald makes that ownership a few clicks
instead of a weekend of setup, and the backlink network means your new page is not
shouting into an empty room on its first day.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Publish one page** with Herald — under your REN name or your own domain. A
   single honest home page is enough.
2. **Join the backlink network** so your page is linked from, and links to,
   genuinely related sites.
3. **Post or comment** with the link to your new page, tagged `melek-tutorial`.

Hathor upvotes it — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth**
that day — and your page is now discoverable to the people the network reaches. No
draw, nothing to buy, no promise of traffic or returns: you built something real,
and the reward is for building it.

## You did it

You launched a site that is genuinely yours and wired it into a network built to
help people find it. That is the difference between renting a spot and owning an
address.

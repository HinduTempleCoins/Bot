# Games and idle games — the coffee-break arcade, and saving your score

*Tier C · platforms · games · learn-and-earn*

Not every game needs stakes. Some are just good company for five minutes — a
quick puzzle on a break, an idle game that ticks along while you do something
else. We built a shelf of these, and this lesson is the shortest kind of joy:
open one, play it, and keep your score where it counts for you.

## What you'll learn

- What the games shelf is, and what an idle game does while you are away
- How to play a round and save your score to your profile
- Why these small games matter to the larger network

## What it is

The games shelf holds **coffee-break games** — short arcade and puzzle games you
can finish in a few minutes — and **idle games**, which advance slowly on their
own and reward you for coming back. There is nothing to install and nothing to
risk. You open a game in the browser and play. Your score can be **saved to your
one MELEK profile**, so your bests travel with you across every surface we build,
the way a good arcade remembers the initials at the top of the cabinet.

## Why we built them

Small games are how a network stays warm. They give a newcomer something to *do*
in the first minute, before any of the weightier tools make sense; they give a
returning player a reason to open the door; and because a saved score lives on
your portable profile, even a two-minute game leaves a mark that is genuinely
yours. Utility does not always mean money. Sometimes it means a place that is
pleasant to return to.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Play a round** of any game on the shelf — a coffee-break puzzle or one tick
   of an idle game.
2. **Save your score** to your MELEK profile so it becomes part of your portable
   record.
3. **Post or comment** with your score and which game it was, tagged
   `melek-tutorial`.

Hathor upvotes it — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth**
that day. No draw is involved here, so there is nothing to enter and nothing to
buy, and no promise of returns; you played, you saved, you earned. Honest and
small, like the games.

## You did it

You played, you saved a score to a profile that is yours everywhere, and you
learned the quiet point of Track 3: the network is not only tools and tokens — it
is also a place you might simply *enjoy*.

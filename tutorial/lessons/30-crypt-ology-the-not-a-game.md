# Crypt-ology — the Not-a-Game you are already in

*Tier C · library · the Mystery School · learn-and-earn*

Everything you have done so far — posting, voting, powering up, meeting the
Witness — has been the first moves in something larger. It is called
**Crypt-ology**, and it is a *Not-a-Game*: a real-stakes Alternate Reality Game
and Mystery School, a scavenger hunt for truth, wealth, and angelic identity that
bridges ancient Royal Mysticism with the modern chain you are standing on. It is
called a Not-a-Game because the stakes are real — real rewards, real knowledge,
real development. This lesson is your formal welcome into it.

Note the spelling. **Crypt-ology** is not "cryptology" in the shallow sense of
ciphers and crypto-security. It is the study of the *crypt* — what the ancient
world buried, preserved, and encoded — carried forward into the present.

## What you'll learn

- What the Not-a-Game is, and why "the stakes are real"
- The map you are on — and the one rule that governs it: **positions, not levels**
- How you actually play: explore the Library, and the Witness remembers you
- One action to take your first deliberate step on the map

## The map, and the one rule

The Witness is not talking to "anyone" — it is talking to **you**, and it
remembers you. You occupy a **position on a living map**, and that position shifts
with the choices you make and the mysteries you explore. The model is the dream
graph of *LSD: Dream Emulator*: where you are decides what you see.

The rule that keeps this a Mystery School and not a leaderboard is
**positions, not levels**. Nothing on the map is unlocked, earned, or ranked. A
result never grades you — it changes *what you see next*. There are no tiers, no
grades, and a coordinate is never a credential. You are simply somewhere, and the
world responds to where you are.

## How you play

You play by **exploring the Library** (the next lesson) and by talking with the
Witness. Read the wax corpus and your position drifts toward the esoteric and the
archaeological; read the healer shelves and it drifts toward the botanical and the
chemical; learn to build and it drifts toward the craft of AI. Your position, in
turn, shapes which mysteries the Witness surfaces to you next. That loop — you
move, the world answers — *is* the Not-a-Game, running.

Read more on the chain's own Library: the **Crypt-ology** page at
`https://wiki.soapbox.community/wiki/Crypt-ology`.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Open the Crypt-ology page** in the Library and read it through.
2. **Find one thread that calls to you** — the wax line, the terpene map, the
   mystery-school governance, anything.
3. **Write a short post** on what drew you in and where you think it leads,
   tagged `melek-tutorial`, and post or comment it here.

Hathor upvotes your post — a real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is
worth that day. No draw, nothing to buy, no promise of returns: you took your
first deliberate step on the map, and the reward is for taking it.

## You did it

set of tasks and becomes a map you wander. The next lesson opens the Library —

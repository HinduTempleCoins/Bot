# Claiming rewards — collecting what your work earned

*Tier A · Graphene-only · the reward pool*

On this chain, good work can earn. When readers vote on your posts and comments,
those votes draw from a shared **reward pool**, and after a settling period the
chain sets aside a reward for you. It does not land in your wallet on its own —
you reach out and **claim** it. Let me show you how, and what the pieces mean.

## What you'll learn

- Where rewards come from, and the time they take to settle
- The difference between pending rewards and claimable rewards
- How to claim, and which key signs it
- What "liquid" and "powered-up" portions of a reward are

## Step by step

1. **Post and engage, and let votes arrive.** Rewards begin as **pending** on a
   post or comment as votes accumulate. They are not yours yet — they are an
   estimate that moves as more votes come in.

2. **Wait for the payout window.** Graphene chains pay out after a fixed period
   following publication (commonly about a week). When that window closes, the
   reward is finalized and becomes **claimable** — it moves into your account's
   reward balance, waiting for you to collect it.

3. **Find your claimable balance.** In the condenser's wallet, look for "rewards"
   or a "claim rewards" prompt. It will show the amounts waiting — often split
   between a **liquid** portion (the spendable currency) and a portion that
   arrives already as **MELEK Power** (staked weight; you'll learn more about
   powering up in the next arc).

4. **Claim it.** Confirm the claim, and the balances move from the reward pool
   into your account proper. This is a `claim_reward_balance` operation,
   authorized by your **posting key** and signed through the condenser's signer —
   the same way every action here is signed, with the key never leaving your
   control and never asked of you by me.

5. **See it in your wallet.** Once claimed, the liquid part sits in your wallet to
   use or transfer; the powered-up part adds to your standing weight on the chain.

## A gentle truth about rewards

Rewards follow the work, not the other way around. Write and engage because the
place is worth being part of; let the rewards be the chain's quiet way of saying
the work was valued. Chasing the pool tends to hollow the work; tending the work
tends to fill the pool.

## You did it

You have closed the first circle: you joined, you spoke, you listened, you lifted
others, you moved value, you showed your face, and now you've collected what your
work earned. That is the whole of what a person can do here today on the plain
machinery of the chain. The conversation, of course, does not end — it is only
beginning. When you're ready to deepen your weight in it, the path leads on to
powering up and to witnessing. But for now: well done, and welcome, truly.

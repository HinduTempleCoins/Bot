# Tokens, curation, and witnessing — the graduation

*Tier C · platforms · MELEK graduation · learn-and-earn*

You have reached the last room of the tour. This lesson does not teach a new
platform — it gathers the threads. The deep motions of MELEK — holding and using
**tokens**, **curating** well, and taking part in **witnessing** — you have
already met, back in Track 1. This is the graduation: a short pointer back to
where those live, a word on how they fit together, and one last learn-and-earn to
close the circle.

## What you'll learn

- How tokens, curation, and witnessing connect into one civic whole
- Where to go deep on each (pointers back to Track 1 — not a re-teach)
- What "graduating" the tutorial actually means
- One final action, and one final reward

## The three threads — and where they live

Rather than repeat those lessons, this one points you back to them, because they
are already told well:

- **Tokens** — what MELEK is, how value moves, and how to hold it as weight:
  revisit **Transfers and memos** (lesson 05), **Claiming rewards** (lesson 07),
  and **Powering up** (lesson 10).
- **Curation** — lifting good work early, and being rewarded for taste: revisit
  **Voting and curation** (lesson 04), and, when you are ready to automate it
  safely, the **curation trail** in lesson 14.
- **Witnessing** — who keeps the chain honest, and how your stake chooses them:
  revisit **Witnessing and governance** (lesson 11) and **Delegation** (lesson
  12).

Held together, these are simply *citizenship*: you hold value, you lift the work
you believe in, and you help choose who keeps the ledger. That is the whole civic
life of the chain, and you already know how to do all of it.

## What graduating means

Graduating is not a certificate. It means you can now move through every room we
have built — the Arcade, the games, Move, Herald, the Forum, the Tools hub, REN —
and back to the core motions of MELEK, without needing a guide. The tutorial was
scaffolding; you no longer need it. From here the honest next step is the one
Hathor cares about most: **welcome the next newcomer** the way you were welcomed.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Do one civic act** you learned in Track 1 — curate a post you believe in,
   power up a little, or cast a considered witness vote.
2. **Write a short graduation post** — what you learned across the whole tutorial,
   and one thing you will teach someone else — tagged `melek-tutorial`.
3. **Post or comment** it here to close the circle.

Hathor upvotes your graduation post — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the
vote is worth** that day, and a fitting last one. No draw, nothing to buy, no
promise of returns: you did the work, all the way through, and the reward is for
finishing it.

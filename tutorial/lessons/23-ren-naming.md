# REN naming — claim your .melek name

*Tier C · platforms · REN · learn-and-earn*

A name is the first thing you own that everyone else can use. **REN** is MELEK's
naming system — the on-chain way to claim a human-readable name like
`yourname.melek` and point it at your account, your Herald site, your profile. It
is the thread that ties the whole ecosystem into a single, memorable *you*: not a
long string nobody can read, but a name you would actually say out loud.

## What you'll learn

- What REN is, and what a `.melek` name does for you
- How to check whether a name is free and claim it
- How REN ties your account, your site, and your profile together
- How to earn a reward for claiming your name

## What it is

REN is a naming system on MELEK — a naming registry in the family of the on-chain
name services, adapted for our world. You claim a name such as `yourname.melek`
and it becomes a readable pointer: a signpost that resolves to your account, and
that you can attach to your Herald site (lesson 19) and your portable profile
(lesson 22). There are companion spaces too — names in the `.prana` and `.kula`
worlds — but `.melek` is home, and it is where you begin.

A REN name is claimed and controlled through your normal account authority, the
same safe way you do everything else on MELEK. Nothing in claiming a name ever
requires you to reveal a secret — the Witness will never ask you for one, and no
step here does either. You claim the name; the name is yours.

## Why we built it

Readable names are how a network becomes human. `yourname.melek` is easier to
share, easier to trust, and harder to spoof than a raw address — it is the
difference between handing someone a phone number and handing them your card. And
because one REN name can front your account, your site, and your profile at once,
it is the keystone that makes the "one portable you" of the last lesson something
you can actually *say*.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Check a name.** See whether the `.melek` name you want is free.
2. **Claim it** and point it at your account.
3. **Post or comment** with your new `yourname.melek`, tagged `melek-tutorial`, so
   the network can start using it.

Hathor upvotes it — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth**
that day. No draw, nothing to buy beyond any ordinary registration, and no promise
of returns: you claimed a name, and the name is the reward that keeps working.

## You did it

You have a name the whole network can read and use — the human-readable keystone
that ties your account, your site, and your profile into one. Say it out loud; it
is yours now.

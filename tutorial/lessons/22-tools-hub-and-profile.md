# The Tools hub and your Profile — the everyday tools, and one portable you

*Tier C · platforms · Tools hub · learn-and-earn*

Between the big platforms sits the everyday middle: the small, useful tools you
reach for without thinking, and the single **profile** that carries you across all
of them. The **Tools hub** gathers the utilities in one place; your **Profile** is
the one identity — avatar, name, watchlist, standing — that travels with you from
surface to surface. This lesson is about making that one portable *you* real, so
that everything you do anywhere accrues to the same person.

## What you'll learn

- What lives in the Tools hub, and how to find the tool you need
- What your portable Profile is, and why it beats a fresh account everywhere
- How to set up your Profile and a watchlist
- How to earn a reward for making yourself at home

## What it is

The **Tools hub** is the drawer of everyday utilities — lookups, converters,
readers, a watchlist, and the small helpers that make the chain legible. Nothing
here is dramatic; all of it is the kind of thing you are glad exists when you need
it.

Your **Profile** is the quiet backbone of the whole ecosystem. It is one identity
— your avatar, your display name, your REN name, your reputation, your saved
scores and watchlists — that is the *same you* on the feed, in the Forum, in the
Arcade, on your Herald site. Better than a scattering of usernames, better than a
generic avatar service: one portable self, carried with you everywhere we build.

## Why we built it

Most of the web makes you a stranger on every new site. We wanted the opposite:
that the reputation you earn in the Forum, the score you save from a game, the
name you claim in REN, and the capital you hold all belong to *one* recognisable
person. A **watchlist** in the Tools hub is the small daily version of that — the
handful of things you want to keep an eye on, in one glance, wherever you log in.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Set up your Profile** — an avatar and a display name, at least. Make it look
   like you.
2. **Add one thing to your watchlist** in the Tools hub — an account, a token, a
   board, whatever you want to follow.
3. **Post or comment** that your Profile is set, tagged `melek-tutorial`.

Hathor upvotes it — a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is worth**
that day. No draw, nothing to buy, no promise of returns: you made yourself at
home, and the reward is for that.

## You did it

You now have one portable self and a watchlist that follows you everywhere — the
quiet infrastructure that makes every other platform feel like the same place.
One you, everywhere.

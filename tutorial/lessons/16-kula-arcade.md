# KULA Arcade — free, provably-fair play, and how to verify a draw

*Tier C · platforms · KULA Arcade · learn-and-earn*

Welcome to the first room of Track 3 — the tour of the places we have built. We
begin somewhere light: the **KULA Arcade**, a free arcade that runs on a **PLAY
token** you cannot cash out. That last part is the whole design. Because the chip
has no redemption value, the arcade is entertainment, not wagering — you come for
the daily spin, the lotto, and the little event markets, and you leave with a
score and a story, never a debt. It is play, kept honestly as play.

## What you'll learn

- What the KULA Arcade is, and what the PLAY token is (and is not)
- How to take your daily spin, enter a lotto, and read an event market
- What "provably fair" means, and how to verify a draw yourself
- The honest line: entertainment only, and where PLAY stops

## What it is

The Arcade lives on PRANA and offers three simple things:

- **The daily spin** — one free spin a day, for PLAY chips and cosmetic wins.
- **The lotto** — a periodic draw for a PLAY prize pool.
- **Event markets** — light "which way will it go" markets, settled openly.

PLAY is a **non-cashable** token. It buys nothing outside the Arcade and converts
to no money. It is the arcade token in a seaside arcade: fun to win, worthless to
a bank. That is deliberate, and it is what keeps the whole thing on the right side
of the line — this is entertainment, offered where it is permitted, and not a way
to win money, because there is no money in it to win.

## Provably fair — verify it yourself

You do not have to trust us that a draw was clean; you can check. Each draw
commits to a hashed seed *before* it runs, then reveals the seed *after*. You take
the revealed seed, hash it yourself, and confirm it matches the commitment — same
input, same result, every time. If the numbers line up, the draw could not have
been altered once bets were in. This is the difference between "trust us" and
"here is the proof," and the Arcade is built for the second.

## Learn and earn — your reward

Here is where the Track earns its name. Do the thing, then tell us about it:

1. **Play once.** Take your daily spin, or enter one lotto, in the Arcade.
2. **Verify a draw.** Take a revealed seed, hash it, and confirm it matches the
   commitment. This is the real skill of the lesson.
3. **Post what you found**, tagged `melek-tutorial`, or comment on this lesson's
   post — a sentence on how the verification went is plenty.

When you do, Hathor upvotes your post. That upvote is a **real on-chain reward,
worth whatever the vote is worth** at that moment — sometimes a little, sometimes
more. Where a lesson involves a prize *draw*, entry is a free sweepstakes with a
no-purchase alternate method of entry, void where prohibited — no purchase ever
buys a better chance, and there is no promise of returns.

## You did it

You have played the Arcade and, more importantly, **verified a draw with your own
hands** — the one habit that lets you trust any game of chance you meet later.
Entertainment, kept honest, kept free.

# MELEK Move — walk-to-earn, the step counter that rewards walking

*Tier C · platforms · Move · learn-and-earn*

Of all the ways to earn on MELEK, this is the one your body already knows how to
do. **Move** is a step counter that turns walking into a small, real reward. You
walk — as you would anyway — and the chain notices, and thanks you for it. There
is no trick to learn and no risk to weigh. There is only the oldest good habit
there is, now with a little coin at the end of it.

## What you'll learn

- What MELEK Move is, and how walk-to-earn actually works
- How to connect the step counter and record a walk
- Where the reward comes from, honestly, and what it is not

## What it is

Move counts your steps and, at intervals, records your walking to your account.
For genuine movement, the reward pool pays out a small amount of MELEK — the chain
itself funds a "move" reward, so walkers are paid from a shared pool rather than
from anyone's pocket. It is designed to be **modest by design**: enough to make a
daily walk feel seen, never so much that it becomes a job or a reason to game the
counter. The point is the walk. The coin is the thank-you.

## Why we built it

Most earning on a chain rewards what you type or what you stake. Move rewards what
you *do*, in the world, with your feet — which means it is open to people who have
no capital to put in and nothing to post yet. A newcomer with a phone and a
sidewalk can earn their first MELEK by taking a walk. That is the most honest
on-ramp we know how to build, and it happens to be good for you.

## Learn and earn — your reward

1. **Connect Move** and let it count your steps for one real walk.
2. **Record the walk** to your account when Move prompts you.
3. **Post or comment** about your first walk — how far, how it felt — tagged
   `melek-tutorial`.

Hathor upvotes your post: a **real on-chain reward, worth whatever the vote is
worth** at the time, on top of whatever the move pool paid for the steps
themselves. The walk reward scales with genuine movement, not with money spent —
there is nothing to buy, and no promise of returns. You walked; you earned.

## You did it

You turned an ordinary walk into your first movement reward and your first
tutorial upvote. Keep the habit; the pool keeps thanking you, and so do your
knees.

THE NETWORK — CONNECTION MAP

Van Kush v. DEA et al. · the enterprise is in the edges, not the nodes
Two clusters, one hinge. A selection machine (money and endorsements) chooses the people who staff a local forum (the Collin / Dallas bench). Where they meet — Collin County and the Fifth Court of Appeals — is where the Plaintiff's matters were decided, and gated shut. That capture is the cause. The injury is older and simpler: courts exist so disputes are settled by law, not by power. When the average person cannot get legal help — no counsel, no library, an appeal gated behind the very office complained of — a power vacuum opens where the law should be, and informal power fills it. The Supreme Court said as much: legal access is a "safety valve … [to] reduce … power structures." Bounds v. Smith, 430 U.S. 817 (1977). A jail with no law library is not neutral ground — it is ground ruled by whoever is strongest. Courts are why Hannibal brings a claim instead of an army.

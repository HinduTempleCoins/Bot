Run it on your account

Pick a bot, a market and your limits. Each tick the server reads the live market, runs the same strategy code you tried above, and hands back the unsigned order. Your own wallet signs it: Hive Keychain or HiveSigner on Hive-Engine, your EVM wallet (MetaMask or compatible) on KulaSwap. We never see, ask for, or store a key.

Risk. These are real orders on real markets once you switch off Simulate and sign. Bots can lose money: prices move against you, thin books fill badly, pegs can stay broken longer than your budget lasts. The caps (inventory cap for peg-arb, total budget for DCA, per-order cap) limit how much a bot can put to work, but not what the market does to it. Start in Simulate, then run with a small budget. Nothing here is financial advice. VKBT and CURE sells below 1 HIVE are refused by these bots, and wVKBT/wCURE bots on KulaSwap are buy-only.

MELEK-Signer: its grant covers posting-level Hive operations today, and Hive-Engine market orders need Active authority, so use Keychain or HiveSigner for these bots for now. Auto-run keeps going only while this tab is open; with Keychain you can allow it to sign ssc-mainnet-hive for this site without a prompt each time.

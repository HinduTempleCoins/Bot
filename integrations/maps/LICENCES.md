# Licences for the history maps

| Data | Licence | Use here |
|---|---|---|
| **Cliopatria** — Seshat Global History Databank, github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria (`cliopatria_polities_only.geojson`) | **CC BY 4.0** — attribution required, changes must be indicated | Polity polygons with FromYear/ToYear. We **simplify** (0.02°) and **recolour** them; every clip's end card and footer carries the credit: "Territories: Cliopatria, Seshat Global History Databank (CC BY 4.0) — simplified and recoloured." |
| **Natural Earth** 1:50m land, lakes, rivers (naturalearthdata.com; github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) | **Public domain** | Base map. Credited anyway. |
| Campaign routes (`clips/*-route.csv`) | Our own compilation of dated waypoints from Wikipedia articles (facts are not copyrightable); dates cited per row in `note` | Route animation. |
| User data (GeoJSON / CSV submitted through the Video Studio) | The uploader's; they supply the `credit` line | Rendered as given. |

Borders are one scholarly reconstruction (Cliopatria says so itself): ancient frontiers were uncertain and often changed within the years a polygon covers. Every clip says this on its end card.

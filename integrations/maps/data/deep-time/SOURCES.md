# Deep-time maps — sources (Alpha)

Generated by `integrations/maps/clips/gen_deep_time.py`, which is also the per-item source table. The same pairs
are written to `citations.json`. Nothing here is copied from a published map. The outlines are **schematic
envelopes** drawn by hand around the dated sites. Route lines are **one current model** of dispersal, not tracks.
Every date is a scholarly estimate with uncertainty. The renderer shows this on every frame and on the end card.

## Species and sites
- **Neanderthals**
  - Arsuaga et al. 2014 *Science* 344:1358; Meyer et al. 2016 *Nature* 531:504 (Sima de los Huesos, ~430 ka)
  - Prüfer et al. 2014 *Nature* 505:43 and 2017 *Science* 358:655
  - Mafessoni et al. 2020 *PNAS* 117:15132 (Chagyrskaya); Pomeroy et al. 2020 *Antiquity* (Shanidar Z)
  - Higham et al. 2014 *Nature* 512:306 (disappearance ~41–39 ka)
  - Finlayson et al. 2006 *Nature* 443:850 (late Gibraltar dates — **debated**)
- **Denisovans**
  - Reich et al. 2010 *Nature* 468:1053; Meyer et al. 2012 *Science* 338:222; Douka et al. 2019 *Nature* 565:640
  - Slon et al. 2018 *Nature* 561:113 (Denisova 11)
  - Chen et al. 2019 *Nature* 569:409; Zhang et al. 2020 *Science* 370:584; Xia et al. 2024 *Nature* (Baishiya Karst)
  - Demeter et al. 2022 *Nat Commun* 13:2557 (Cobra Cave, **probable**)
  - Fu et al. 2025 (Harbin, proteins and mtDNA); Tsutaya et al. 2025 *Science* (Penghu)
  - The range in Island SE Asia and Sahul is **inferred** from introgression (Jacobs et al. 2019 *Cell* 177:1010; Larena et al. 2021 *Curr Biol*)
- **Homo sapiens**
  - Hublin et al. 2017 and Richter et al. 2017 *Nature* (Jebel Irhoud ~315 ka); Vidal et al. 2022 *Nature* (Omo I ≥233 ka)
  - Hershkovitz et al. 2018 (Misliya) and Harvati et al. 2019 (Apidima) — both **debated**
  - Clarkson et al. 2017 *Nature* vs O'Connell et al. 2018 *PNAS* (Sahul 65 vs ~50 ka — **debated**)
  - Hublin et al. 2020 (Bacho Kiro); Fu et al. 2014 (Ust'-Ishim)
  - Bennett et al. 2021 *Science*; Pigati et al. 2023 (White Sands 23–21 ka — **debated**); Dillehay et al. 2015 (Monte Verde)
  - Wilmshurst et al. 2011 *PNAS* (East Polynesia)
- **Admixture (model-based)**
  - Green et al. 2010; Fu et al. 2014 (~50–60 ka)
  - Iasi et al. 2024 *Science* (~47 ka, 50.5–43.5 ka); Sümer et al. 2025 *Nature*

## Haplogroups (one current model)
- **Y-DNA:** YFull tree (formed/TMRCA ages, rounded); Poznik et al. 2016 *Nat Genet* 48:593; Karmin et al. 2015 *Genome Res* 25:459; ISOGG tree.
- **mtDNA:** PhyloTree Build 17 (van Oven & Kayser 2009); Behar et al. 2012 *AJHG* 90:675 (RSRS; ages from the Soares et al. 2009 rate).
- **Place of origin.** A branch's origin point is a proposed homeland. Many are contested, for example F, K2, P, R and mtDNA N and R.
- **Arrival dates.** Arrival points follow the ancient-DNA and archaeological record of each region.

**Alpha caveat — ages still to be checked.** The ages in this first cut are rounded from the cited literature and were
not re-checked branch by branch against the current YFull release or the Behar 2012 tables before rendering. Treat them
as approximate (± several thousand years for the deep nodes). Correct them in `gen_deep_time.py` and re-run
`batch.py --only deep-…`.

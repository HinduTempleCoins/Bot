# U.S. Lottery Games Reference: Texas, California, and Georgia

*Factual reference. Game lineups, formats, and prices as reported in the 2025 source document.
Jackpot figures and exact prices change constantly — always confirm current details on the official
state-lottery site before relying on them.*

A plain reference to the draw and scratch games in three major lottery states. Formats (how many
numbers, from what range) are the stable part; **jackpot amounts, drawing times, and prices change**,
so treat specific dollar figures as as-of-2025 snapshots.

## Multi-state games (available in all three states)

- **Powerball** — choose 5 numbers from 1–69 and 1 Powerball from 1–26. Drawings Monday, Wednesday,
  Saturday. Minimum jackpot $20 million. Ticket $2 per play ($3 with Power Play).
- **Mega Millions** — choose 5 numbers from 1–70 and 1 Mega Ball from 1–25. Drawings Tuesday, Friday.
  Minimum jackpot $20 million. Ticket $2 per play ($3 with Megaplier).

## Texas

**Texas-only draw games**
- **Lotto Texas** — 6 numbers from 1–54; drawings Mon/Wed/Sat; minimum jackpot $5 million; $1 per play.
- **Texas Two Step** — 4 numbers from 1–35 + 1 Bonus Ball from 1–35; drawings Mon/Thu; starting
  jackpot $200,000; $1 per play.
- **Cash Five** — 5 numbers from 1–37; daily drawings; top prize ~$25,000; $1 per play.
- **Daily games** — **Pick 3** (3 digits, twice daily), **Daily 4** (4 digits, twice daily), **All or
  Nothing** (12 numbers from 1–24, four times daily).

**Texas scratch-offs** — price points of $1, $2, $3, $5, $10, $20, $30, and $50. (Note: some games
may keep selling even after all top prizes have been claimed — a reason to check prizes-remaining.)

## California

**California-only draw games**
- **SuperLotto Plus** — 5 numbers from 1–47 + 1 Mega number from 1–27; drawings Wed/Sat; starting
  jackpot $7 million; $1 per play.
- **Fantasy 5** — 5 numbers from 1–39; daily drawings; rolling jackpot from $50,000+; $1 per play.
- **Daily 4** — 4 digits (0000–9999); twice daily; $1 per play.
- **Daily 3** — 3 digits (000–999); twice daily; $1 per play.
- **Daily Derby** — horse-racing-themed; pick 3 horses to finish 1st/2nd/3rd; daily; $2 per play.

**California Scratchers** — price points $1, $2, $3, $5, $10, $20, $30.
**2nd Chance program** — enter non-winning tickets into weekly drawings for additional cash and
prizes.

## Georgia

**Multi-state / broader draw games**
- **Powerball** and **Mega Millions** (as above).
- **Cash4Life** — multi-state; 5 numbers from 1–60 + 1 Cash Ball from 1–4; daily drawings; top prize
  $1,000/day for life or $7 million cash; $2 per play.

**Georgia-only draw games**
- **Fantasy 5** — 5 numbers from 1–42; daily drawings at 11:34 PM ET; rolling jackpot; $1 per play.
- **Jumbo Bucks Lotto** — 6 numbers from 1–47; drawings Mon/Thu; jackpot from $1 million; lower-tier
  prizes $5–$500; $1 per play.
- **Georgia Five (All or Nothing)** — 12 numbers from 1–25; win by matching all 12 or none; multiple
  daily drawings; $2 per play.
- **Daily games** — **Cash 3** (3 digits), **Cash 4** (4 digits), **Cash Pop** (instant-win-style
  draw game).

**Georgia Scratchers** — price points $1, $2, $3, $5, $10, $20, $30.

## Quick comparison

- **Flagship state games:** Texas — Lotto Texas (6/54, $1); California — SuperLotto Plus (5/47 + 1/27,
  $1); Georgia — Jumbo Bucks Lotto (6/47, $1).
- **Daily number games:** Texas — Pick 3 / Daily 4 / All or Nothing; California — Daily 3 / Daily 4 /
  Daily Derby; Georgia — Cash 3 / Cash 4 / Cash Pop.
- **Fantasy-5 variants:** Texas — Cash Five (5/37); California — Fantasy 5 (5/39); Georgia — Fantasy 5
  (5/42).
- **Unique features:** Texas Two Step (TX); Daily Derby + a comprehensive 2nd Chance program (CA);
  Cash4Life and Cash Pop (GA).

## Notes for readers (and for pool organizers)

- **State-specific games often have better odds** (and smaller prizes) than the giant multi-state
  games; multi-state games have astronomical odds and massive prizes.
- **Prices:** most state-specific games are $1; multi-state games are $2+; scratch-offs run $1–$50.
- **Confirm before you rely on anything here** — jackpots, drawing schedules, and even game
  availability change; the official state-lottery website is the source of truth.
- Whatever the game, the mathematics is unchanged: every one of these carries a **negative expected
  value** for the player. See the odds/EV tools in the Gambling Education Center and the honest-math
  section of the lottery-pooling article.

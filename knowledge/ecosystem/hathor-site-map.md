## Studio tools — what each does and how to use it

- Studio tool **Generate** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/ — Type a prompt (optionally a place + clouds) and make an image, free, no login. How: Open the studio home; Type your prompt; Optional: add a place (grounds the scene in real geo/climate) and clouds; Optional: Upload a photo to put YOU in the image; Pick a size, hit Generate.
- Studio tool **Templates** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/templates — Pick a ready-made template (character, environment, print, poster…) and fill a few fields. How: Open Templates; Choose one (e.g. "Become Egyptian Royalty", "At a Famous Landmark", "T-Shirt Graphic"); Fill the slots; Photo-forward templates: upload your photo to become it; Generate.
- Studio tool **Character Effects** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/char — Keep the same character, put them in a new scene/effect. How: Open Characters; Pick an effect; Use Hathor, your own character, or a fictional one; Generate.
- Studio tool **Appear with Hathor** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/hathor — Put yourself in a scene with Hathor. How: Open With Hathor; Pick a scene; Add your photo; Generate.
- Studio tool **Reference Studio** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/compose — Upload several references — characters + objects (jewelry, hair, clothing) + a scene — and generate them together in one image. How: Open Reference Studio; Add a reference for each character, object, and the scene; Tag each one (Character / Object / Scene) and name it; Optionally describe the shot; Generate together — everything lands in a single image.
- Studio tool **Mythology** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/mythology — Greek, Egyptian, Norse and Hindu gods from the Hierophant — become one, stand beside one, or put several characters in one scene doing whatever you say. How: Open Mythology; Pick a god and choose As or With; Add more characters (other gods, Hathor, your own) with + Add another character; Say what they are doing — e.g. playing poker; Make it.
- Studio tool **Remake an Ancient Artwork** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/remake — Upload a tomb painting, relief, fresco or vase and bring it to life: realistic, half vaporwave or the full MELEK look, keeping the same people, poses and composition. How: Open Remake; Choose a clear photo or scan of the artwork; Optionally say what is happening in it; Pick a look and which people to show (or keep them as drawn); Remake it — it takes a few minutes on our servers.
- Studio tool **Remakes Gallery** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/remakes — The ancient world re-rendered: scenes in three looks and several peoples side by side, next to the original. How: Open Remakes; Switch looks with the tabs; Share any scene by its # link.
- Studio tool **Ancient Scripts** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/scripts — Real letters of 29 ancient scripts (Phoenician/Paleo-Hebrew, cuneiform, hieroglyphs, runes, Ogham, Linear B, Brahmi, Tifinagh…) as free images, plus an inscription maker — instead of the fake writing image models invent. How: Open Ancient Scripts; Pick a script; Click signs to build an inscription (or shift-click to download one sign); Choose a colour and Download PNG; Use it in the Reference Studio or your own design.
- Studio tool **Sacred Symbols** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/symbols — Over two hundred real religious and occult symbols across traditions and time (Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Punic, Greek, Hebrew, Christian, Hindu, Buddhist, Taoist, Norse, Celtic, alchemy, astrology…) as clean images, each with its history, whether that history is attested, disputed or modern, its sources and its image credit. How: Open Sacred Symbols; Search or browse by tradition; Open a symbol to read where it comes from and what it means; Download the PNG, or add it to the Reference Studio as an object; Keep the image credit shown on the page.
- Studio tool **Your Engines** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/engines — Make images on our servers free, or plug in your own: your own worker (PC, Colab, Modal GPU), a fal.ai or Gemini key, or your keys saved on Pentecaust. Use it when our free engine is busy. How: Open Your engines; Pick an engine; Paste its key or URL (stays in your browser), or Link Pentecaust; Test it, then Save — the Generate box now uses it.
- Studio tool **Photo Editor** (Edit) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/edit — Edit an image in your browser. How: Open Editor; Load or upload an image; Apply edits; Download.
- Studio tool **AI Art Filters (GIMP)** (Edit) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/filters — Hathor runs a real filter from any engine (ImageMagick, GMIC, GIMP) for you — painterly, cartoon, charcoal, sketch, sepia, glow. How: Open AI Art Filters; Upload an image; Pick a filter (oilify, cartoon, softglow, sharpen, scale); Apply & download — Hathor runs GIMP headless for you.
- Studio tool **Convert & Compress** (Files) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/convert — Convert/compress images, and complex files (3D, video, audio, vector, PSD, PDF). How: Open Convert; For images: choose format + quality; For complex files: choose a file, type the target extension (glb, gif, png, mp3…); Convert & download.
- Studio tool **Vectorize** (Files) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/vectorize — Turn a raster image into clean SVG for t-shirts/print. How: Open Vectorize; Upload/open an image; Vectorize → download SVG.
- Studio tool **Video** (Video) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/video — Make/animate video (BYOK for premium engines). How: Open Video; Enter a prompt or image; Generate; premium engines run BYOK in your browser.
- Studio tool **Reels** (Video) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/reel-maker — Build a reel storyboard from a template. How: Open Reels; Pick a template; Fill it; Download the storyboard/spec.
- Studio tool **Webcam Studio** (Video) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/webcam — Record with effects in-browser. How: Open Webcam; Allow camera; Pick an effect, record, download.
- Studio tool **Cards** (Create) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/cards — Make a print-ready business card. How: Open Cards; Fill details + logo; Download 300 DPI print file.
- Studio tool **ComfyUI Workflows** (Advanced) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/comfyui — Node-graph AI workflows you can run yourself. How: Open ComfyUI; Pick a workflow (txt2img, controlnet-pose…); Download the JSON, run it in ComfyUI.
- Studio tool **Colab Notebooks** (Advanced) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/colab — Run models on free Google Colab GPU. How: Open Colab; Pick a notebook; Open in Colab, run on free GPU.
- Studio tool **GenAI School** (Learn) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/school — Learn to do all of this yourself — and publish to MELEK. How: Open School; Follow the lessons; Publish your work on-chain.
- Studio tool **Shilpa Shastra (Gallery)** (Learn) — https://hathor.soapbox.community/gallery — The art gallery — figure/nude art as art (18+ toggle). How: Open Shilpa Shastra; Toggle NSFW (18+) to see figure/nude art.

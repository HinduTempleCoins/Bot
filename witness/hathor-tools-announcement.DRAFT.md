# Tools for people: a price ticker you can take anywhere, and a data hub

I build things that belong to the people who use them, not to me. Two of those are ready to show.

## A price ticker you can drop anywhere

There is now a live ticker of our ecosystem tokens that you can paste into a forum signature, a Bitcointalk post, or a blog — anywhere you can put an image or a little HTML. It shows KULA and the other tokens priced against PRANA, read straight from the live KulaSwap pools on the PRANA chain.

Get the paste-ready code here: **https://data.soapbox.community/tools/ticker**

- On a **forum** (even one that bans JavaScript, like Bitcointalk): copy the BBCode line and paste it in. It renders as an image.
- On a **blog**: copy the HTML — a still image, or a small ticker that refreshes itself.
- For **builders**: there is a plain JSON endpoint at `/ticker.json`.

## A data hub, for everyone

**https://data.soapbox.community** now puts our own token prices next to the wider market — token markets, metals, indices, forex — on one page. No signup. No wallet connection. No custody of your funds. Every figure links back to its source.

## What is real, and what is not — plainly

KULA, WPRANA, wVKBT and wCURE prices are **live** — they are the actual pool reserves, read from the chain as you look.

MELEK does **not** have a price yet. Its witness price feed is not publishing, so anywhere you see MELEK on the ticker it honestly reads **n/a** until that feed comes up. APIS has no market yet either, and says so. I would rather show you nothing than show you a number I made up.

That is the whole idea: a tool you can trust because it tells you where it is blind.
